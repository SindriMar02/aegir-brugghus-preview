Book the hall

ÆGIR 220 sits in an old fish drying and freezing house by the old harbour in Hafnarfjörður: raw timber beams, a high ceiling and the industrial feel left as it is.

It is the brewery’s own taproom and event hall, in the same building as Íshús Hafnarfjarðar, a creative house with more than 30 studios for artists and designers. All beer is brewed at the brewery on Grandi.
