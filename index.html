ÆGIR BRUGGHÚS was founded in 2017 on Grandi, Reykjavík’s old harbour area. Every beer on tap at ÆGIR 101 and ÆGIR 220 is brewed here: lagers, IPAs, dark beers and seasonal beers. ÆGIR is also on tap in restaurants across the city centre.
